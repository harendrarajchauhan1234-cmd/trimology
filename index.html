Trimology™ Ingredients

Trimology™ is marketed as a gut-focused metabolism-support formula. The ingredient list can vary across websites, but the most consistently listed core ingredients are RS2 resistant starch, chicory-root inulin, Clostridium butyricum, Akkermansia muciniphila, and Bifidobacterium infantis.

1

Pharmaceutical-Grade Resistant Starch Type 2 (RS2)

RS2 is a type of resistant starch that passes through the upper digestive tract largely undigested and reaches the colon, where it can serve as a food source for certain beneficial gut bacteria. Trimology™ uses RS2 as a central component of its gut-support approach, with the goal of supporting digestive health and healthy metabolic function.
2

Chicory Root Extract / Inulin

Chicory root provides inulin, a prebiotic fiber that can nourish beneficial bacteria in the gut. Inulin is commonly used to support digestive regularity and a healthy gut microbiome. In Trimology™, it is paired with resistant starch and probiotics as part of the formula's gut-health strategy.
3

Clostridium butyricum

Clostridium butyricum is a probiotic microorganism associated with the production of butyrate, a short-chain fatty acid produced by gut bacteria. Trimology™ includes this strain to support a balanced intestinal environment and digestive wellness.
4

Akkermansia muciniphila

Akkermansia muciniphila is a gut bacterium that has received research interest for its relationship with the intestinal mucus layer and metabolic health. Trimology™ uses it as part of its targeted probiotic blend intended to support healthy gut and metabolic function.
5

Bifidobacterium infantis

Bifidobacterium infantis is a probiotic strain commonly associated with digestive and intestinal health. In Trimology™, it is included to complement the other probiotic strains and help support a balanced gut microbiome and comfortable digestion.
